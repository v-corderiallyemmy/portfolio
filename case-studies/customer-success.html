CASE STUDY • CUSTOMER SUCCESS

Building customer strategy from the ground up.

CORL Technologies • Cybersecurity SaaS

Helping build the systems behind a growing Customer Success organization through customer health, QBR strategy, retention programs, reporting, and lifecycle design.

Customer Success Retention Customer Health QBR Strategy HubSpot Power BI Change Management
30% Reduction in client churn across the organization
Founding Member of the Customer Success team

01

The Opportunity

As a founding member of CORL's Customer Success function, I had the opportunity to help shape how the organization approached the customer lifecycle.

That meant going beyond account management and building repeatable ways to understand customer health, identify risk, communicate value, manage renewals, and create consistent experiences as the organization scaled.

The goal: move from reactive customer management toward a structured, measurable customer success operating model.
02

Building a Customer Health Strategy

I researched customer health models and explored how engagement, relationship quality, product usage, business outcomes, and risk signals could be translated into a more useful view of account health.

Customer Signals → Health Visibility → Risk Identification → Action

CRM

Recommended and supported HubSpot-driven processes to improve customer data visibility and lifecycle management.

Business Intelligence

Explored Power BI as a way to improve customer health reporting and surface trends for internal decision-making.

Customer Success Technology

Piloted ChurnZero to evaluate how a dedicated Customer Success platform could support health, engagement, and retention workflows.

03

Redesigning the QBR Experience

Rather than treating a QBR as another account status call, I helped develop a more intentional approach centered on customer value.

TRADITIONAL APPROACH

Status & Updates

Health conversations, contract discussions, operational updates, and business reviews can easily blur together.

VALUE-BASED APPROACH

Outcomes & Strategy

Separate the QBR from routine health and contract conversations and focus the meeting on what the customer considers meaningful.

CORE PRINCIPLE
Don't assume what value means to the customer. Ask them what data, outcomes, and insights are meaningful to their business.
04

Building a Retention Strategy

I also piloted a structured retention approach designed to help the team identify behavioral and relationship risks earlier and respond more consistently.

01 Identify risk signals
02 Understand root cause
03 Create corrective actions
04 Align ownership
05 Monitor progress
06 Adjust strategy
05

Supporting Organizational Change

As the organization evolved its Customer Success model into a broader Customer Experience function, my role evolved with it.

I became a senior operational resource for the team, supported associate development and training, managed escalations, and helped maintain customer continuity through organizational change.

Following a workforce reduction, I also supported transition efforts while serving as a point of escalation for client relationships.

06

Business Impact

As structured customer success practices matured across the organization, CORL experienced a 30% reduction in client churn along with stronger customer engagement.

The outcome was organizational rather than the result of any single initiative, but it reinforced the value of creating consistent health, retention, reporting, and engagement practices across the customer lifecycle.

THE TAKEAWAY

Customer success becomes scalable when good instincts become systems.

The strongest customer programs combine relationships with repeatable processes, meaningful data, clear ownership, and a shared definition of customer value.
