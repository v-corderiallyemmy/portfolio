CASE STUDY • RISK & GOVERNANCE

Building stronger controls into everyday operations.

GitHub Education • Contract

Strengthening eligibility verification, renewal governance, documentation, reporting, data quality, and program integrity across a large-scale education program.

Risk Management Governance Operational Controls Salesforce Program Integrity Process Design
2,500+ Institutions within audit scope
40% Reduction in unnecessary renewal volume
Standardized Verification documentation
Proactive Program integrity approach
01

The Challenge

Large programs require more than efficient processing. They also require consistent standards for determining eligibility, maintaining accurate data, documenting decisions, and identifying potential program integrity concerns.

As I became more familiar with GitHub Education's operational workflows, I identified opportunities to strengthen controls throughout the program lifecycle.

The principle: governance works best when it is built into normal operations rather than added after something goes wrong.
02

Strengthening Onboarding Governance

Consistent Verification

Standardized onboarding notes and documentation practices to create a clearer record of verification decisions.

Decision Quality

Helped clarify operational criteria so that eligibility decisions could be made more consistently across cases.

Data Integrity

Improved the quality and consistency of information captured during program administration.

03

Improving Renewal Governance

Renewal processes are an important control point because they determine whether existing participants continue to meet program requirements.

OPPORTUNITY

More Structured Review

Renewal workflows had opportunities for stronger consistency, documentation, and prioritization.

IMPROVEMENT

Clearer Governance

Refined workflows and eligibility guidance to support more intentional review and more consistent renewal decisions.

IMPACT
The improved approach contributed to a 40% reduction in unnecessary renewal volume while strengthening program integrity.
04

Improving Data Quality & Operational Visibility

Governance depends on trustworthy information. I worked to improve reporting, documentation, and operational data quality so teams had a clearer view of program participation and decision history.

This included improving Salesforce reporting, standardizing operational notes, and creating more structured issue documentation.

05

Moving From Reactive to Proactive Risk Management

Program integrity work also surfaced the need for a broader review of participating institutions.

I developed an approach for reviewing more than 2,500 institutions and identifying opportunities to strengthen preventative controls going forward.

THE TAKEAWAY

Good governance should make good operations easier.

My approach to operational risk focuses on embedding clear standards, documentation, data quality, and preventative controls directly into program workflows.
