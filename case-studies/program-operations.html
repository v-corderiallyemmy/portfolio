CASE STUDY • PROGRAM OPERATIONS

Turning complex operations into scalable systems.

GitHub Education • Contract

Modernizing documentation, support workflows, reporting, operational standards, and escalation processes to create a more consistent and scalable program.

Program Management Process Improvement Zendesk Salesforce Change Management Customer Experience
72% Reduction in open ticket backlog
40% Reduction in unnecessary renewal volume
20% Increase in Campus Program enrollment
5 mo. Promoted into team leadership
01

The Situation

When I joined GitHub Education, several core operational processes depended on documentation and workflows that had not kept pace with how the program was actually operating.

SOPs and internal repositories were outdated, customer response templates were stored manually, onboarding practices varied, renewal workflows required additional structure, and support tickets could remain open beyond expected response windows.

The opportunity: create clearer systems without slowing down a high-volume global program.
02

Standardizing Program Operations

Documentation

Reviewed existing SOPs and operational resources, identified gaps, and updated processes supporting onboarding, renewals, eligibility, and ongoing program administration.

Operational Clarity

Worked with management to clarify ambiguous processes and translate decisions into repeatable workflows the team could consistently follow.

Team Enablement

Created quick-start resources and standardized documentation to make training, onboarding, and day-to-day decision-making easier.

Escalation Management

Established clearer ownership, prioritization, and escalation pathways for complex or high-impact cases.

03

Modernizing Customer Support Operations

BEFORE

Manual & Fragmented

Customer response templates were maintained outside the support platform, creating extra steps and making consistency harder to maintain.

AFTER

Integrated & Repeatable

Revised customer-facing templates for clarity, completeness, and tone, then transitioned them into Zendesk macros once platform access was available.

RESULT
Clearer ownership, prioritization, escalation pathways, and support workflows contributed to a 72% reduction in the open ticket backlog.
04

Creating a Continuous Improvement Model

Rather than treating process documentation as static, I approached operational improvement as a cycle.

01 Identify friction
02 Investigate the cause
03 Design the process
04 Document the standard
05 Enable the team
06 Measure & improve
05

Growing Into Program Leadership

Five months after joining the program, I was promoted into a lead role supporting day-to-day program operations.

My responsibilities expanded to include workload prioritization, team onboarding and mentorship, complex escalations, process improvement, operational reporting, and cross-functional coordination.

The role reinforced one of the principles that continues to shape my approach to program management: strong operations are not just about completing work. They are about building systems that help people make better, faster, and more consistent decisions.

THE TAKEAWAY

Scalable programs need scalable operating systems.

My approach combines process design, documentation, customer experience, data, and change management to create systems that can grow with the program they support.
