About Me

I build structure where complexity lives.

I currently support GitHub Education as a contract Lead Education Administrator and Program Operations Lead, helping manage and improve the systems behind global education programs.

Across both environments, the common thread has been translating ambiguity into practical systems that improve the experience for customers, teams, and leadership.
